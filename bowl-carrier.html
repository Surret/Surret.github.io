Hybrid Structural Bowl & Carrier System

Designing a true one-handed hybrid carrier combining a central steel load core with parametric 3D-printed sliding dovetail interlocks to navigate gated partitions safely.

The client required a specialised, single-handed carrier system capable of transporting two bowls and two drinks simultaneously while navigating a gated partition. The engineering needed to accommodate two crucial constraints: keeping one hand entirely free to operate physical gate latches, and managing dynamic liquid/solid load balance without tipping or structural deflection.

Key Challenges & Solutions

  • Hybrid Structural Framework: To bypass the tensile limitations of standalone 3D-printed plastics under sustained cantilever loads, I engineered a hybrid assembly. A central steel pole was integrated to handle primary vertical load-bearing forces, shifting mechanical stress away from the printed components.
  • Mechanical Dovetail Interlocks: To secure the bowl-retaining rings to the central hub without relying on weak chemical adhesives, I designed custom sliding Dovetail Joints in Fusion 360. This mechanical joint distributes shear stress across the physical geometry of the printed plastic layers, creating a highly rigid, reliable lock.
  • Modularity & Ergonomics: The interface components were parametrically modelled, ensuring the design remains fully modular. The entire system was built for rapid assembly, teardown, and scaling to suit varying container diameters in the future.

Personal Learning

  • Load Isolation in Hybrid Assemblies: Gained experience combining traditional metallic hardware with additive components to isolate mechanical stresses and overcome material tensile limitations.
  • Print-Toleranced Mechanical Joints: Refined clearance tolerances in Fusion 360 for sliding dovetails to achieve smooth assembly without loose play or layer shearing.

Tools & Specifications

  • Design Platform: Fusion 360 (Parametric & Tolerance Design)
  • Manufacturing Process: FFF / FDM 3D Printing
  • Material Selection: Premium PLA (Formulated as a high-strength skin over the internal skeleton)
  • Joint Architecture: Slide-locking Mechanical Dovetails
  • Hardware Integration: Heavy-duty central steel load core, dual screw-mounted cup-stabiliser rings

Final Deliverable

The completed four-point carrier system successfully transitioned from a conceptual prototype into a highly dependable, daily-use tool. Combining traditional metallic hardware with custom-sliced additive manufacturing allows the user to navigate secure gates confidently with zero deflection, zero load failure, and complete physical safety.
