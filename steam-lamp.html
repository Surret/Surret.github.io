The Steam Lamp Project

Designing custom period-accurate headlamps and tail lights for a 1/3 scale miniature steam locomotive, merging authentic aesthetic styling with functional lighting integration.

This project focused on engineering functional headlamps and tail lights for a 1/3 scale miniature steam locomotive. The primary objective was to craft housings that served as reliable working fixtures while mirroring the vintage, industrial aesthetic of full-scale steam machinery.

Designing bespoke enclosures for a scaled vehicle required balancing internal optical components, long-distance wiring harnesses, and slicing optimisations to produce clean 3D-printed geometries without internal post-processing difficulties.

Key Challenges & Solutions

  • Masking Layer Lines: Standard FFF print lines break the illusion of cast metal. By applying a tuned "fuzzy skin" modifier in Cura, the exterior surfaces were given a randomized matte texture that mimics cast iron and eliminates visible layer lines.
  • Self-Supporting Internal Geometry: Due to the hollow design and steep internal overhangs, standard auto-generated tree supports collapsed or fused inside the top cavity. The internal roof was redesigned with a 45-degree angled chamfer, making the structure completely self-supporting and removing the need for internal support material entirely.
  • Component Integration & Remote Powering: The lamp housing holds the LED array and optical lens, while electrical wiring routes back through the chassis to a central battery and switch unit at the driver's cab. For the tail lights, a custom red diffusion cup was 3D printed to convert standard white output into appropriate rear illumination without needing dedicated coloured LEDs.

Personal Learning

  • Design for Supportless Printing: Incorporating draft angles directly into CAD CAD geometries eliminates support removal labor, preserves surface finish inside hollow parts, and reduces print failure rates.
  • Modular Light Filtering: Using printed translucent diffusion filters provides an economical way to adapt standard cold-white light sources into custom colours without re-wiring or stocking multiple LED variants.

Tools & Specifications

  • 3D Modelling & CAD: Fusion 360 (Self-Supporting Chamfers & Lens Retention)
  • 3D Printer: Creality Ender 3 V2
  • Slicing Software: Ultimaker Cura (Fuzzy Skin & Surface Modifiers)
  • Primary Material: High-Density PLA Filament (Black)

Future Possibilities

  • Standalone Commercial Design: Miniaturise and integrate a self-contained rechargeable battery circuit directly inside the lamp body, eliminating tethered wiring and enabling easy mounting across wider model engineering applications.
