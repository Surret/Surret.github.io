Engineering Approach & Process

"I build practical, real-world solutions to mechanical and electrical challenges. Good engineering isn't about fluff—it's about structural integrity, functional tolerances, and reliable electronics."

1. Problem Definition & CAD

Every project begins with a real-world constraint, from dynamic load paths to electrical timeouts. I translate physical measurements into precise, parametric 3D models in Fusion 360, designing for assembly and stress distribution from day one.

2. Prototyping & Bench Testing

I move quickly from CAD to physical hardware using 3D printing and bench assembly. Prototypes are tested under actual operational conditions—evaluating timing loops, stress points, and mechanical fitments to catch flaws early.

3. DFM & Hardware Integration

The final iteration is optimised for long-term durability. This means refining print geometries, integrating strain relief, wiring solid-state electronics, and delivering hardware that works reliably in the field.
