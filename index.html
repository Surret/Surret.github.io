My Philosophy & Process

"I believe in using systems to empower people. My purpose is to create products and processes that are simple, clear, and functional. I believe that elegant design is not about what you add, but what you take away."

1. Ideation & Planning

Every project begins with a simple idea. I start with sketching and digital modelling to define the scope and feasibility. This phase is crucial for identifying potential challenges early on.

2. Prototyping & Iteration

I use 3D printing to quickly create physical prototypes. This allows for hands-on evaluation of the design, and I'm not afraid to make multiple versions until the form and function are perfect.

3. Refinement & Finalisation

The final stage involves optimising the model for the best possible print quality and finishing the product. This includes post-processing techniques like sanding, painting, or assembly to bring the creation to life.
