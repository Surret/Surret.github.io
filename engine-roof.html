The Engine Roof Project

Translating an existing paper design into a precise CAD model for manufacturing and creating a custom power bank holder.

This project involved creating a new roof for the miniature steam engine. While the overall design and measurements were pre-existing on paper, my role was to translate these 2D drawings into a precise 3D CAD model. This digital model was essential to get the roof professionally built by a joiner. In addition, I designed a custom power bank holder that would seamlessly integrate with the new structure.

A key part of the project was the creation of a custom holder for a power bank. This component was designed to securely hold the power supply for the LEDs and was a crucial functional addition to the roof.

Key Challenges & Solutions

  • Translating 2D Drawings to CAD: The primary challenge was accurately converting detailed measurements from paper drawings into a precise 3D CAD model for a joiner to manufacture. This required careful attention to detail, especially when working with the limitations of the free version of the software, to ensure the final product would fit perfectly.
  • Designing a Custom Power Bank Holder: With the roof's dimensions set, I had to create a holder that would securely contain the power bank and fit into the final structure. This required careful design to ensure the holder was strong enough and had a clean, finished look.

Personal Learning

  • Translating 2D to 3D for Manufacturing: I learned how to convert detailed 2D paper drawings into a precise 3D CAD format. This is a crucial skill for bridging the gap between traditional design methods and modern manufacturing processes, especially when working within the constraints of free software.
  • Integrating Custom Components: This project taught me how to create a custom component—the power bank holder—that integrates seamlessly with a larger, pre-existing structure. It's a valuable lesson in designing for real-world constraints.

Tools & Technologies

  • 3D Modelling: Fusion 360
  • 3D Printer: Creality Ender 3 V2
  • Slicing Software: Cura
  • Materials: PLA Filament (Black)

Future Possibilities

  • Integrating Electronics: A potential next step is to design a more integrated system for the electronics. The original intention was to add switches within the power bank holder to enable switching between two power banks and to be able to turn the lights on or off from the cab. While this was not implemented due to time constraints, it remains a potential future improvement. The current wiring is already concealed within grooves in the design, maintaining the aesthetic of a steam-powered engine. This could be further improved by creating a built-in charging port on the holder and a more permanent, integrated connection to the LEDs.
