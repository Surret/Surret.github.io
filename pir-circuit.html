Smart-Fountain PIR Control Module

Engineering a universal inline solid-state gatekeeper module with a parametric acrylic-viewport enclosure to eliminate pet fountain pump burnout.

Standard pet water fountains operate on a constant 100% duty cycle, running the pump continuously. This leads to rapid mechanical wear, premature motor burnout, and unnecessary power consumption. The objective was to engineer a universal, non-invasive "gatekeeper" module that automatically activates the pump only when a pet is in physical proximity, extending hardware lifespan significantly.

Key Challenges & Solutions

  • Transparent Inline Power Integration: To maintain complete system modularity, the control circuit sits directly on the external power line between the DC supply and the fountain. This transparent integration ensures the solution is universal, interfacing with any standard DC pet appliance without modifying or damaging the original product's internal wiring.
  • Parametric Enclosure & Multi-Material Integration: Modeled entirely in Fusion 360, the housing features a custom parametric design with a specialized recessed bezel. This bezel secures an integrated acrylic viewport, enabling real-time visual inspection of the circuit logic and LED indicators without compromising the enclosure seal.
  • Solid-State Switching Architecture: The internal array couples a Passive Infrared (PIR) motion sensor with a logic-level MOSFET operating as a solid-state switch. This removes mechanical relay wear, delivering silent and instantaneous power activation.
  • Collaborative Prototyping: The electrical assembly was executed via a structured "Lead/Assist" framework, leveraging high-reliability wiring practices for clean routing, solid solder joints, and compact strain relief.

Personal Learning

  • Multi-Material Tolerancing & Fits: Gained experience designing precise parametric bezels in Fusion 360 to seat clear acrylic windows flush into 3D printed (PLA) housings.
  • Solid-State Power Control: Practical application of logic-level MOSFETs instead of electromechanical relays to achieve wear-free, silent switching for inductive DC loads.

Tools & Specifications

  • 3D Modelling & CAD: Fusion 360 (Parametric Assembly & Bezel Tolerancing)
  • Fabrication Process: FFF / FDM 3D Printing
  • Primary Material: High-Density PLA (Brown)
  • Secondary Material: Precision-Cut Acrylic (Integrated Viewport Layer)
  • Core Components: HC-SR501 PIR Motion Sensor Module, Logic-Level Switching MOSFET, Inline USB/DC Power Interconnects

Final Outcome

The finished module converts standard pet fountains into automated smart appliances. Introducing a reactive duty cycle governed by solid-state switching eliminates pump burnout completely, delivering a modular, plug-and-play unit that can be hot-swapped onto matching DC appliances in seconds.
